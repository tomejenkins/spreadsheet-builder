An interactive eight-week scenario with department-level volume forecasts, cross-training transfers, overtime, and hiring lead times. Compare simple baseline forecasts and inspect how assumptions change recommendations.

Public demo, not a production optimization system. Synthetic data only. Hiring is a peak-demand heuristic; transfers are applied in the displayed order; overtime is allocated to remaining gaps. Forecasts use simple statistical baselines, not machine learning. Validate results before operational use.

What becomes the client product?

This engine can be reused behind authenticated client workspaces. A production version will need file/API imports, saved scenarios, shift calendars, employee-level skill eligibility, department minimums, training ramp-up, wage differences, audit logs, permissions, and validated optimization. Forecast models should be selected only after backtesting on actual historical data.
